# Compliance Readiness Draft

Engineering draft only. Legal review is required before commercial launch, public testing, app store submission or customer use.

## Google Play

- Prepare Data Safety declaration.
- Provide privacy policy URL.
- Complete content rating.
- Minimise permissions.
- Use internal testing first.
- Confirm account deletion requirements if accounts are created.

## Future Apple App Store

- Prepare App Privacy labels.
- Use TestFlight before release.
- Review Sign in with Apple requirements if third-party login is offered.
- Confirm iOS SDK and Xcode readiness.

## AI Disclosure

- Explain when recommendations are rules-based and when AI wording is used.
- Do not imply certainty.
- Show confidence and data gaps.
- Do not invent facts.

## Privacy And Consent

- Private by default.
- Consent before precise location, photo upload, analytics or AI provider processing.
- Future export/delete flows required.
- GDPR/UK GDPR readiness requires legal review.

## Location Data

- Approximate labels should be enough for many MVP workflows.
- Precise coordinates should be opt-in.
- Public sharing of spot pins must not be introduced by default.

## Fishery And Welfare Disclaimers

- Users must follow fishery rules and local law.
- The app must warn against disturbing spawning fish.
- Recommendations do not replace user judgement or fish care.
- The app never guarantees catches.
