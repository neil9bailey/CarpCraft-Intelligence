# Privacy Policy Draft

Engineering draft only. Legal review is required before commercial launch, public testing, app store submission or customer use.

## Data The App May Collect

- Account data such as display name and email.
- Venue, swim and spot records.
- Approximate or precise location if the user chooses to add it.
- Session logs, rod setups, baiting records and observations.
- Catch and blank records.
- Photos if photo upload is enabled later.
- Water readings and weather snapshots.
- Recommendation inputs, outputs, confidence scores and outcomes.
- Device, diagnostics and analytics data if enabled later.

## Private By Default

Venues, swims, spots, catch locations, target fish notes and photos should be private by default. Public sharing is not part of the MVP.

## Location Data

Precise location should only be collected after explicit user action. Approximate labels should be supported where exact coordinates are unnecessary.

## AI Processing

Future AI features may process structured session data and retrieved knowledge snippets. AI must not be used to invent private venue facts. Users should be able to understand when AI processing is enabled.

## Third-Party Providers

Future providers may include authentication, cloud hosting, weather APIs, analytics, crash reporting, AI providers and app store platforms. Provider details must be completed before launch.

## User Rights

Future production versions should support data export and deletion. GDPR/UK GDPR readiness requires legal review.

## Retention

Retention rules are not finalised in Phase A. Sensitive angling data should not be retained longer than needed for user-visible product value and legal obligations.
