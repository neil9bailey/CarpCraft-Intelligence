# Terms Of Use Draft

Engineering draft only. Legal review is required before commercial launch, public testing, app store submission or customer use.

## Product

CarpCraft Intelligence is a private angling log and watercraft assistant. It helps users record venues, sessions, observations, catches, blanks and environmental readings.

## No Catch Guarantee

Recommendations are informational and evidence-ranked. They do not guarantee bites, catches, fish locations, fish behaviour or angling success.

## User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

- Following fishery rules and local law.
- Practising safe and ethical angling.
- Protecting private venue and target fish information.
- Avoiding disturbance of spawning fish.
- Checking recommendations against real conditions.

## Account And Data

Future account features may allow users to create, export or delete data. Exact account terms require legal review.

## AI And Recommendations

AI or rules-based outputs may be incomplete or wrong. Users must not treat output as professional, legal, environmental, veterinary or safety advice.

## Commercial Terms

Subscription, trial, refund and cancellation terms are not finalised in Phase A.
